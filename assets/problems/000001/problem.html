Time limit per test: 2 seconds Memory limit per test: 256 megabytes There are buildings in front of you with heights [a_0,a_1,...,a_{n-1}], where a_i is an integer. Assume each building has a depth of 100 units, there is a 10 unit gap between each of them, they are all directly in front of you, and the ground is flat. The first building is 100 units in front of you, and your eyes are 6 units above ground level. How many buildings are visible?

Constraints

  • 10 <= a_i <= 10^8
  • 1 <= n <= 10^7

Inputs

  • first line: n
  • second line: the n integers of a

Examples

Input 1: 

6
10 47 500 1000 2783 4616
Output 1: 
6
Input 2: 
8
100 187 100 1000 1250 10000 1238 47100
Output 2: 
4
