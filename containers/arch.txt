architecture
============

each submission runs in its own container. 

bind mounted directories into the container should have least privileges.

the follwing are bind mounted in:

- /code:ro
- /inputs:ro
- /outputs:rw

containers are responsible for:

- building the program
- executing the program
- saving the outputs

containers don't know the correct answers. 

containers have a timeout for total time to stop excessive build times and handle any other transient issues that may occur within the container.
